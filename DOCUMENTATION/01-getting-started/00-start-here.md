# Start Here

Four steps to go from zero to contributing. Do them in order — each one unlocks the next.

1. **Install your tools** — [software-installation.md](software-installation.md). Git, Python, and VS Code. ~20 minutes.
2. **Learn Git & GitHub** — [github-beginner-guide.md](github-beginner-guide.md). How to clone this repo, make changes, and open your first pull request.
3. **Find your group and check the calendar** — read [groups-and-attendance.md](../02-events-and-activities/groups-and-attendance.md) (Foundations or Advanced, plus attendance rules), then [upcoming-events.md](../02-events-and-activities/upcoming-events.md) for what happens each week.
4. **Start your project** — [group-project-collaboration.md](group-project-collaboration.md). Form your team, get your project branch, and learn the work loop you'll use every session.


Stuck at any point? Ask in the club channel or bring it to the next session — don't burn hours alone on a setup problem.
