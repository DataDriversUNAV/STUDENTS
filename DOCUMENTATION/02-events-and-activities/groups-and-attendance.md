# Groups & Attendance

Every member belongs to a numbered group, from 1 to 10. Everyone meets in the same weekly session and follows the same program, and each group works together on one project.

## Your group

If you don't know your group number, ask the coordinator. Your number gives you your folder and your branch. For example, Group 3 works in `PROJECTS/Grupo_3/` on the branch `grupo-3`. [group-project-collaboration.md](../01-getting-started/group-project-collaboration.md) explains how to work together step by step.

## What we follow

- **Course:** DataCamp's [Data Analyst in Python](https://app.datacamp.com/learn/career-tracks/data-analyst-with-python) career track. It has 9 courses of about 4 hours each, and you get a Statement of Accomplishment when you finish.
- **How it works:** finish the week's DataCamp course at home before the next class. In class, a mentor explains the concepts and solves the exercises with you, then you get time to practice and ask questions.
- **Your group's project:** a data-analysis project on a dataset your group chooses. You'll clean it with pandas, explore it, visualise it and run one hypothesis test. You propose it in week 7 and present it at the final showcase.

## Sharing sessions

Every 3rd week the regular class is replaced by a joint sharing session (see [upcoming-events.md](upcoming-events.md)):

- Each group shows its course progress and that cycle's project milestone, and gets feedback.
- **Upload your materials before the session** to your group's folder in [PROJECTS/](../../PROJECTS/README.md).

## Attendance rules

- Attendance is taken at every session, including sharing sessions.
- **3 absences = you're removed from the club.** The coordinator will warn you after your 2nd absence.
- There are no sessions during exam periods or official breaks, so those weeks never count.
- Every group must present its project by the final showcase.

Can't make a session? Tell the coordinator before it starts.
