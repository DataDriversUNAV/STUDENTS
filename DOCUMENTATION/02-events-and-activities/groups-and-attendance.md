# Groups & Attendance

Data Drivers has two groups this year. Everyone meets in the same weekly session, but each group has a different goal and a different kind of project.

## Foundations — for non-programmers

- **Who:** you have little or no programming experience.
- **What you follow:** DataCamp's [Data Analyst in Python](https://app.datacamp.com/learn/career-tracks/data-analyst-with-python) career track. It has 9 courses of about 4 hours each, and you get a Statement of Accomplishment when you finish.
- **How it works:** finish the week's DataCamp course at home before the next class. In class, an Advanced mentor explains the concepts and solves the exercises with you, then you get time to practice and ask questions.
- **Your project:** a data-analysis project on a dataset you choose. You'll clean it with pandas, explore it, visualise it and run one hypothesis test. You propose it in week 7 and present it at the final showcase.

## Advanced — projects & investigation

- **Who:** you can already program in Python.
- **Teaching:** you'll be on a mentor rota. When it's your turn, you explain that week's Foundations course and solve its exercises live, then help Foundations members during work time.
- **Your project:** an investigation project, done alone or in a team of up to 3. It covers a research question, data, analysis and a write-up. You present your materials at every sharing session.

## Placement

In week 1, tell the coordinator which group you're joining. If you want Advanced, you'll do a short check of a few Python/pandas exercises. Foundations members can move up to Advanced at the semester break once they've finished the first 4 courses and their pandas mini-analysis.

## Sharing sessions

Every 3rd week the regular class is replaced by a joint sharing session (see [upcoming-events.md](upcoming-events.md)):

- Advanced members present their investigation materials so far and get feedback.
- Foundations members show their course progress and that cycle's project milestone.
- **Upload your materials before the session** to your project folder in [PROJECTS/](../../PROJECTS/README.md).

## Attendance rules

- Attendance is taken at every session, including sharing sessions.
- **3 absences = you're removed from the club.** The coordinator will warn you after your 2nd absence.
- There are no sessions during exam periods or official breaks, so those weeks never count.
- Everyone, Foundations and Advanced, must present one project by the final showcase.

Can't make a session? Tell the coordinator before it starts.
