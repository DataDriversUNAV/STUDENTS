# Upcoming Sessions — Academic Year 2026-2027

This is the full calendar for the year, week by week. We meet **once a week** (day and time to be confirmed: Wednesday 19:00 or Thursday 15:00–18:45). Bring your laptop with [Python, Git, and VS Code installed](../01-getting-started/software-installation.md).

Every member is in a numbered group. Read [groups-and-attendance.md](groups-and-attendance.md) for how groups and attendance work.

Everyone follows DataCamp's [Data Analyst in Python](https://app.datacamp.com/learn/career-tracks/data-analyst-with-python) career track. That's 9 courses: you do one per class week at home, and in class we explain it and solve the exercises.

The weeks run in a 3-week cycle:

- **Class weeks (2 of every 3):** mentors explain the week's course, then everyone works on the exercises and their group's project.
- **Sharing weeks (every 3rd week):** there's no regular class. All groups meet and show their progress.

Week dates are Monday–Friday. The session is on whichever day we confirm.
