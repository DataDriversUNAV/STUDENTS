# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This club exists to help students learn programming and data analytics together. We want it to be a welcoming, respectful space for members of all backgrounds and skill levels — including complete beginners.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with members who are new to programming or Git/GitHub.
- Give constructive feedback on pull requests and project reviews — critique the work, not the person.
- Credit others' contributions and avoid submitting someone else's work as your own.
- Keep discussions (issues, PR comments, meetings) on-topic and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Dismissive or discouraging comments toward beginners.
- Plagiarizing project work or misrepresenting contributions.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, report it to the current president or vice president (see [team-roles.md](ADMIN_ROLE/DOCUMENTATION/01-club-info/team-roles.md) for who's currently in that role). Reports will be handled discreetly and taken seriously.

## Enforcement

Officers may address violations through a private conversation, a formal warning, or — in serious or repeated cases — removal from the club and its repository.
